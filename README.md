## Entry — "Our Exit Strategy Is Flying Away"

> We turn dropped crumbs into recurring revenue — zero CAC, since humans throw our inventory at us. $50K for 10% of the company, and our exit strategy is flying away.

One line, 164 characters. A city pigeon's elevator pitch to investors for its startup: the crumb business, told in the pigeon's own voice, in straight venture-capital language — until the exit strategy gives the game away.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) validates the line — one line, 1–200 characters, no leading or trailing whitespace, no control characters — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline and verifies the round trip.
